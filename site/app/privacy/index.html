Privacy

This app runs in your browser. You sign in with Google; Google gives this tab an access token. The token is kept for this tab only and is sent only to Google's own Drive address. Your files go between your browser and Google. They do not pass through any server of ours.

  • We run no server, set no cookies and use no analytics or tracking.
  • We keep no copy of your files, names or token. Closing the tab ends the session; Disconnect also asks Google to revoke the token.
  • The app asks only for access to files you pick or that it creates (drive.file). See the scopes page.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time at your Google account permissions page.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
